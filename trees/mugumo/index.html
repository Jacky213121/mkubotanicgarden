About this tree

The Mugumo is recognised as a culturally important fig tree in Kenya. The exact scientific name must be confirmed from the tree specimen or the MKU Botanic Garden’s records before this page is published as authoritative.

This prototype deliberately avoids guessing the species. Once identification is verified, the page can carry the approved botanical description, ecological significance and cultural history.

What the final page will explain

  • How to recognise the tree by its leaves, bark, crown and fruit.
  • Its ecological relationship with birds, insects and other wildlife.
  • Its cultural significance, presented respectfully and with credible sources.
  • Its location in the garden and any conservation information.

Sources and review

Botanical content should be checked by the garden curator, a botanist or an approved academic reviewer before public release. References will be listed here rather than hidden inside the video.
